Our work

Drag the handle to see the colour come through. Both of these were done in one four-hour sitting, with no bleach past the mid-lengths.

Eleven people, one chair each

Lumière opened above a bakery on Waterfield Road in 2016 with two chairs and a kettle. We have grown since, but the rule has not changed: your colourist is yours. You should never have to re-explain your hair, your scalp or the wedding you are growing it out for.

We are deliberately slow. Colour appointments are booked with real time around them, so nobody is rushed through a toner to make room for the next head. If a service will not suit your hair, we will tell you before you are in the chair rather than after.

  • Open since 2016 — a team of eleven, seven of them senior colourists
  • Wella and Schwarzkopf certified, with annual retraining
  • Patch tests offered free, 48 hours before any colour service
  • Fully sanitised tools per client, and a separate kit for scalp-sensitive guests
